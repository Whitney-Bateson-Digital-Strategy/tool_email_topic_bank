Pick one service, program, or package. If you help a wide range of people, just think of one type of client while you fill this in — you can always come back and run this again for another.

Bucket 1

Outcomes

The big transformation. What do people feel, achieve, or become at the end of it — not the sessions themselves, the result.

Bucket 2

Loved features

What people love about the service itself — the mode, the method, the moments in how you actually work together.

Bucket 3

Hesitations

What's holding people back — the beliefs and barriers in their head before they say yes. (Skip “money” — go one layer deeper.)
